# Contributing to Neuro-OSI

Thank you for your interest. Neuro-OSI is a conceptual framework, so contributions are mostly ideas, critique and design proposals rather than code.

## Welcome contributions

- **Threat scenarios**: new attacks on any layer, and how the architecture should respond.
- **Critique**: where the model is wrong, naive or incomplete.
- **Design proposals**: handshake protocols, policy models, codec ideas, hardware safety mechanisms.
- **Neuroscience input**: what is realistic, what is not, and why.
- **Legal and ethical perspectives**: neurorights, regulation, consent models.
- **Translations** of the documentation.

## How to contribute

1. **Discuss first.** Open an issue describing your idea or critique.
2. **Propose changes.** Fork the repository, edit the relevant document in `docs/`, and open a pull request.
3. **Keep it clear.** Plain language, short sections; use tables and Mermaid diagrams where they help.
4. **Cite sources** for factual claims about neuroscience, security research or law.

## Ground rules

- Respect the core principles in the [README](README.md). Proposals that weaken human control over one's own mind should explain clearly why and what compensates for it.
- Be constructive and respectful.
- This is a speculative framework. Do not present it as a description of real products.
